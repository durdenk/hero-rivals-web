## Store Listing

App name :
Odyssey Arena

Short description (maks 80 char) :
Fight rival heroes in a sword-and-shield survival arena.

Full description (maks 4000 char) :
Enter the arena and prove that you can survive until the end.

Odyssey Arena is an action arena game built around sword-and-shield combat. Step into a compact battlefield where rival heroes fight for survival, react to hits, lose health, and fall one by one until only the last fighter remains.

Fight in fast melee battles, watch your opponents clash around the arena, and look for the right moment to strike. Every match is about positioning, timing, and staying alive while the fight unfolds around you.

Features:

- Sword-and-shield arena combat
- Rival AI fighters in the same battlefield
- Health, hit reactions, and elimination-based survival
- Fast matches designed for mobile play
- A focused action experience without complicated setup

Odyssey Arena is currently focused on a clean arena combat experience. More gameplay depth, characters, combat polish, and progression features may be added as the game grows.
